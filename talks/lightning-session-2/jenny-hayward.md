The Government of Indonesia (GoI) is interested in developing new industrial
hubs that support the use of local capabilities, including workforce and
skills, renewable energy resources, land and infrastructure. CSIRO has
developed an online modelling tool to support the GoI in decision-making on the
supply of energy infrastructure to meet the demand of new industrial hubs. The
model optimises for least-cost supply of energy (electricity and hydrogen),
within constraints such as land, renewable energy resources, transmission and
government policy. The tool will be demonstrated during the presentation, for a
case study site in Indonesia, Batam Island.
