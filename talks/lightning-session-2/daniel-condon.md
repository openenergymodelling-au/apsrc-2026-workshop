This talk will cover a new open-source dataset, developed by the AEMC and hosted by the CSIRO, which encompasses projected renewable output and demand across the National Electricity Market (NEM) based on 85 years of historical weather data; from 1945 to 2025. This dataset was developed using machine learning techniques on historical reanalysis weather data and AEMOs operational market data, and is aimed at helping researchers, modellers and policy-makers understand the impact of weather on dispatch outcomes and reliability in the NEM.
