Building shared energy models requires agreement on what the data and
equations mean, which assumptions are appropriate, and how those decisions can
evolve. Drawing on my experience with collaborative distribution-system
modelling, I will discuss how communities work towards that agreement and
encourage people to contribute. Topics include making modelling choices
explicit, turning disagreements into reviewable proposals, and creating useful
entry points through examples, data, documentation and technical review. The
discussion will consider how to balance a coherent shared specification with
different research needs, and how to turn initial interest into sustained
participation. These questions offer practical starting points for
strengthening collaboration across the Australian open-source energy modelling
community.
